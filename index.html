ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Breaking Data Silos in Land Governance

Land information is often distributed across different departments and government systems. LandGov GIS provides a unified interface for discovering and visualizing this information.

The platform is designed around interoperable APIs and geospatial data so that different land-related datasets can work together.
