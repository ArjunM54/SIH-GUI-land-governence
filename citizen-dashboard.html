❓ Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions, tracking guidance, and citizen assistance.

Frequently Asked Questions
Q: How do I submit an owner-change request? Navigate to Applications → Submit Application → Owner Change. Provide parcel details, new owner information, supporting documents, and submit.
Q: How long does verification take? Applications undergo verification across 5 government officer departments (Cadastral, RoR, Registration, Land Use, Property Tax). Typically completed within 3 to 7 working days.
Q: Why are multiple officers reviewing my request? LandGov enforces multi-departmental governance to prevent fraudulent land transfers. All 5 departments must verify legal title, survey boundaries, deeds, zoning, and tax clearance.
Q: How can I track my application status? Go to Applications → Track Application to view the step-by-step 5-department verification timeline.
